# 📋 Survey Questionnaire — Screen Time, Sleep & Student Life

**Topic:** How screen time affects sleep, stress, and academic performance
among Bangladeshi university students.

**How to use:** Copy these questions into a Google Form (or any survey tool).
Each response becomes one row in `data/survey_responses.csv`.

| # | Question | Type | Options / format |
| - | -------- | ---- | ---------------- |
| 1 | Your age | Number | 18–25 |
| 2 | Gender | Multiple choice | Female / Male |
| 3 | University | Multiple choice | UIU / DU / BUET / NSU / BRAC University / JU / CU / RU |
| 4 | How many hours per day do you spend on screens (phone, laptop, TV)? | Number | hours, e.g. 6.5 |
| 5 | Of that, how many hours on social media? | Number | hours, e.g. 3.0 |
| 6 | How many hours do you sleep per night? | Number | hours, e.g. 7.0 |
| 7 | How many hours do you study per day? | Number | hours, e.g. 4.0 |
| 8 | Your current CGPA | Number | 2.00–4.00 |
| 9 | How stressed do you feel these days? | Scale 1–5 | 1 = not stressed, 5 = very stressed |
| 10 | Do you feel addicted to your phone/social media? | Yes / No | Yes / No |
| 11 | How is your sleep quality? | Multiple choice | Poor / Fair / Good |
| 12 | Do you use your phone in bed before sleeping? | Yes / No | Yes / No |
| 13 | How many days per week are you physically active? | Number | 0–7 |

## ⚠️ Ethics note

- Participation is voluntary and anonymous — no names, emails, or phone numbers.
- The sample data in this repo is **synthetic** (generated, not real people).
- Never publish identifiable answers from a real survey.
